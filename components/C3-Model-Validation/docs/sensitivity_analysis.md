# Sensitivity of the decisions to weights, thresholds and bands

Generated by `scripts/sensitivity_analysis.py`. Each sample model was validated once; its stored measurements were then re-scored and re-judged under each perturbed configuration with the gate's own functions. Nothing was re-measured.

**This analyses the sample models only. It shows how stable the recommendation is under these perturbations; it does not show that the thresholds are correct.**

## How to read this

- A model whose decision survives nearly every perturbation is robust to the arbitrary choices. A model that flips under small changes sits near a boundary, and its decision should be treated as uncertain.
- Critical rules (leakage, selection-rate disparity, F1 floor, unloadable model) depend on thresholds only through the FAIL status, not on the weights or the bands. That is why weight and band changes cannot rescue a model such as `leaky`.
- `MQS range` is taken over the weight, threshold and missing-policy perturbations; the band perturbations do not change the score.
- The perturbations are a design of this study (a 0.1 weight grid, +/-10% and +/-20% threshold distances, bands in steps of 5). Other designs would give other numbers.
